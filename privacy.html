Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026 · Русская версия

1. Who we are

Foxurrent. (“we”, “us”) operates this website as a portfolio and status board for our Roblox games.

2. What data we process

  • No visitor accounts are required to browse the site.
  • localStorage / sessionStorage may store language preference, cookie consent, admin session, and content managed in the browser (projects, posts, team, images).
  • Roblox API requests fetch public game metadata only. We do not receive your Roblox password through this site.

3. Cookies & storage

See our Cookie notice. A consent banner is shown until you choose Accept or Essential only.

4. Third parties

Hosting providers and Roblox may process technical data (e.g. IP) under their own policies.

5. Your choices

Clear site data in your browser to remove local storage. Do not put personal data in public post fields.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. The date above will change when we do.
